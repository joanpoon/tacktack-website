Good to know

Viewings

Viewings are free and by appointment, and take about 30 minutes. We'll share the address when you book. A viewing doesn't hold a piece, so if you've fallen for it, a deposit is the way to make sure it's still there.

Pickup

Pickup is by appointment too. We'll agree a time on WhatsApp and share the address when you book.

When you message

The WhatsApp button on each product page fills in the item name and link for you. If you're writing from scratch, paste the product page link or tell us the item name (e.g. AKARI 45D) so we can find it straight away.
