The story

Yoshitomo Nara

Yoshitomo Nara (born 1959 in Hirosaki, Aomori) is a Japanese artist.

He studied art in Japan and then in Germany. His wide-eyed children look sweet and a little defiant at the same time.

Punk and folk music run through his work, and you can feel it in the attitude of his figures.

We picked two brand-new prints that bring that attitude into a small space. Each is 28 × 36 cm and unframed.

If you'd like them ready to hang, we also have a white solid wood frame made for these prints.
