The story

Verner Panton

Verner Panton (1926–1998) was a Danish designer who loved bold colour, round forms and rooms designed as one whole.

He designed the Flowerpot lamp in 1968: two half-spheres facing each other, very much of its time.

&Tradition makes the Flowerpot today, including the VP9, a small portable version that runs without a cable once charged.

We like it on a desk, a bedside table or a dinner table, in whichever colour makes you smile.
