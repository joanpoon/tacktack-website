The story

About DULTON

DULTON is a Japanese homeware brand known for taking everyday, utilitarian objects and making them more fun to live with. The Double Face Clock borrows from the two-sided clocks hung in train stations and public buildings, redrawn as a low rectangle for homes with lower ceilings.
