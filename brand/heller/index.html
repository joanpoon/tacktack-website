Heller

The story

Anna Dawson for Heller

Anna Dawson is a RISD-trained designer and California surfer. Heller spotted her Swell prototype at WantedDesign during NYCxDesign in 2022, and a year later launched it at ICFF, in collaboration with MoMA. Its wave-shaped curves have since picked up a Domino decor award (2023) and an Apartment Therapy Small/Cool award (2024). Swell Mini followed in 2025.

What's the difference between Swell and Swell Mini?

Size and use. Swell has three waves: the crests catch coats, bags and caps, and the troughs hold keys and sunglasses. Swell Mini is a single wave, part hook and part catchall bowl, for a tote by the door or glasses by the bed. Sizes: Swell (W 73 × D 7.6 × H 35.6 cm, HK$980) in White, Red Orange and Blue; Swell Mini (W 19.7 × D 7.6 × H 16.5 cm, HK$280) in Red Orange, Cream and Blue.
