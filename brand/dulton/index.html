About DULTON

DULTON is a Japanese homeware brand known for taking everyday, utilitarian objects and making them more fun to live with. The Double Face Clock borrows from the two-sided clocks hung in train stations and public buildings, redrawn as a low rectangle for homes with lower ceilings.

Questions about DULTON

Where can I buy the DULTON Double Face Clock in Hong Kong?

At Tack, tack!, an independent design shop in Hong Kong. We currently list Double Face Clock Rectangle (W 44.2 × D 9.8 × H 18.7 cm, HK$1,328) in Grey and Green, all brand new. To buy, message us on WhatsApp (+852 6507 0791) or email hello@tacktackstudio.com. Pickup is by appointment, or we deliver anywhere in Hong Kong.

Does the DULTON Double Face Clock tick?

No. It has a sweep movement, so there's no ticking. The dial is held by magnets for easy time-setting and battery changes, and it runs on 2 × AA batteries.

Is the DULTON clock shipped from Japan, and do I need to drill?

Yes, it's shipped from Japan. Rental-friendly: hang it on a removable adhesive hook or stand it on a shelf, no drilling needed.
