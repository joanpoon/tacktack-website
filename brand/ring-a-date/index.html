Questions about Ring-a-Date

What is different about the Cafè Mousse Limited Edition?

It comes with a special metal plaque for this collection. Only 999 pieces in this Special Collection. Each piece is unique and numbered, with an anti-counterfeiting hologram. HK$1,580, brand new, with box.

How do the coloured rings work?

The coloured rings come in sets of 3 at HK$128, for the Ring-a-Date calendar. The colours are Broom Yellow, Neon Green, Warm Red and Pumpkin Orange.

How do I buy a Ring-a-Date calendar on WhatsApp?

Message us on WhatsApp (+852 6507 0791) or email hello@tacktackstudio.com and tell us which calendar or ring set you would like. We will share payment details, and extra photos if you would like them. A 30% deposit reserves every order. See how to buy.

How does delivery work in Hong Kong?

Showroom pickup is by appointment (we share the address when you book), or we deliver anywhere in Hong Kong, quoted before you pay. Small pieces (lamps, clocks, objects) can go by courier or local delivery. Large furniture goes by movers, quoted separately. Details are on the delivery page.
