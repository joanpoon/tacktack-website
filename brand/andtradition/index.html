The story

About &Tradition

&Tradition is a Danish design brand. These lamps are Verner Panton's Flowerpot VP9, a portable lamp from 1968; the Mist AP15 pendant by All the Way to Paris; and the Journey SHY2 wall lamp by Signe Hytte, from 2018.

Questions about these &Tradition lamps

Where can I buy a Flowerpot VP9 in Hong Kong?

At Tack, tack!. Five portable VP9 lamps are in stock: Swim Blue in Good at HK$1,180 (three minor marks), Swim Blue in Excellent at HK$1,280, Mustard in Excellent at HK$1,280, and brand-new Matt Black and dark green at HK$1,580 each, with the box. Message WhatsApp +852 6507 0791. An earlier Swim Blue has already sold; that page stays up with no price.

What is the difference between the two Swim Blue lamps?

They are two separate pieces. TT-ANT-0001 is Good at HK$1,180, with three minor marks. TT-ANT-0013 is Excellent at HK$1,280 and looks clean. Both are ex-display, with a charger and without a box.

Does the Mist pendant include a bulb, and can you install it?

Yes. This Mist AP15 includes the cord and a bulb. It is HK$2,480, about 22% under the HK$3,190 retail. Installation is an add-on, quoted on WhatsApp. The price is for one pendant.

What is wrong with the Journey wall lamp?

It is Good, at HK$1,340, half of the HK$2,680 retail. There is a small hairline crack on the opal glass near the base join. The photos do not show it clearly. WhatsApp us for a close-up. Installation is an add-on.
