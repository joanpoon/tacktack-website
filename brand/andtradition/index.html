Flowerpot VP9 in Hong Kong

Verner Panton designed the Flowerpot in 1968. The VP9 is the portable lamp: lacquered polycarbonate, Ø16 × H 29.5 cm, with a built-in LED and a touch dimmer. It charges by USB. These pre-loved and second-hand Flowerpot VP9 lamps in Hong Kong are graded, so you can see the condition beside the price. The condition guide sets out the five grades.

Questions about Flowerpot VP9

What do the condition grades mean?

Each piece has one of five grades, shown next to the price. Brand new: unused, never installed. Display: never used at home, shown in a showroom or shop. Excellent: lightly used, and it works perfectly. Good: honest wear consistent with age; sound and fully working. Fair: clear wear, a repair or a replaced part, described and priced to match. The full wording is on the condition guide.

What is the difference between Brand new and a pre-loved Flowerpot?

Brand new means unused and never installed. Flowerpot VP9 · Matt Black is Brand new, with box, charging cable included, at HK$1,580. Flowerpot VP9 · Dark Green is Brand new, with box, charging cable included, at HK$1,580. Mist Pendant AP15 is Brand new at HK$2,480. Pre-loved Flowerpot lamps are graded. Flowerpot VP9 · Swim Blue · Good is HK$1,180, with three small marks, two on the shade top and one on the base. Flowerpot VP9 · Swim Blue · Excellent is Excellent, at HK$1,280. Flowerpot VP9 · Mustard is Excellent, at HK$1,280. Journey Wall Lamp SHY2 is Good, at HK$1,340, with a small hairline crack on the opal glass near the base join.
