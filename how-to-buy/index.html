Holds, deposits & viewings

Every piece at Tack, tack! is pre-loved or limited, so here's how we keep things fair.

  • Holds need a deposit. Messages, likes and comments don't reserve an item. It's yours once we've received your deposit or full payment. First paid, first served.
  • Deposit and hold length by price:
Item priceTo reserveHold length
Under HK$1,500Full payment (no holds)Pick up or deliver within 7 days
HK$1,500 – 9,99930% depositUp to 7 days
HK$10,000 and above20% deposit (min. HK$3,000)Up to 14 days
  • Deposits are non-refundable if you change your mind, but they go 100% toward your purchase. If the item turns out to be materially different from our photos and description when you view it, or we can't hand it over, we refund your deposit in full.
  • The balance is due before pickup or delivery, by FPS, PayMe, bank transfer, cash or PayPal (for overseas buyers). Items are released once payment clears.
  • Installation: Installation service available. Ask us for details.
  • Pickup & delivery: self-pickup is by appointment. Large furniture goes by movers, quoted separately and paid with the balance. Please check lift and stair access and the measurements. If a hold expires without payment, the item goes back on sale and the deposit is kept.
  • Viewings: free, by appointment (30 min). A viewing doesn't hold an item, so place a deposit first if you don't want to miss it.
  • Multi-unit items (e.g. several of the same lamp) follow the same rules per unit.
  • No returns. All sales are final, so ask us anything and we'll send extra photos and measurements before you buy. Nothing here affects your rights under Hong Kong law.

Pieces are offered on all our channels (the website, Instagram, Threads and WhatsApp) at the same time. If something sells before we confirm your order, we refund you in full within 2 working days.
