How to buy & holds Delivery & pickup FAQ Contact
Where is Tack, tack! based?

In Hong Kong. We sell online and over WhatsApp rather than from a shop floor: pickup is by appointment and we share the address when you book, or we deliver anywhere in Hong Kong.

Are your pieces new or second-hand?

Both. We sell curated design classics from around the world, new and pre-loved, and every listing shows its condition grade, from Brand new to Fair.

How do I buy something?

Tap the WhatsApp button on the piece you like. We'll confirm availability, send extra photos if you'd like, and share payment details. Once your payment or deposit arrives, it's yours.

Can you hold a piece for me?

Yes, with a deposit. Under HK$1,500 we take full payment; HK$1,500–9,999 needs a 30% deposit (held up to 7 days); HK$10,000+ needs 20% (min. HK$3,000, up to 14 days). Messages and likes don't hold items.

Which payment methods do you accept?

FPS, PayMe, bank transfer and cash, plus PayPal (for overseas buyers). We don't take card payments on the site, and there's no checkout on the site.

What do the condition grades mean?

We use five steps: Brand new, Near mint, Excellent, Good and Fair. Each listing adds notes on any marks, wear or missing parts. Ask and we'll send close-ups.

Can I see a piece before buying?

Yes. Viewings are free and by appointment (about 30 minutes). A viewing doesn't hold the item, so place a deposit if you don't want to miss it.

Do you deliver?

Yes, anywhere in Hong Kong. Small pieces can go by courier; large furniture goes by movers. We quote before you pay.

Do you offer installation?

Yes. Installation service available. Ask us for details.

Where do I pick up?

Pickup is by appointment. We'll agree a time on WhatsApp and share the address when you book.

Will lamps work in Hong Kong?

Hong Kong runs on 220V with UK-style plugs. For every lamp we'll tell you exactly which cord set, fitting and bulb are included, and whether you need an adaptor or an electrician. Just ask before you buy.

Do lamps come with a cord?

Each listing says so. Our Isamu Noguchi AKARI shades are sold without the cord set: a cord add-on or installation is available, and we quote it on WhatsApp. More AKARI questions are answered on the AKARI page.

Can I return an item?

All sales are final, which is why we're generous with photos, measurements and answers before you buy. If a piece is materially different from its description when you view it, we refund your deposit in full. This doesn't affect your rights under Hong Kong law.

Why did a piece sell while I was asking?

We sell on the website, Instagram, Threads and Carousell at the same time, and the first paid order wins. If something sells before we confirm your order, we refund you in full within 2 working days.

When do new pieces arrive?

Every Thursday (Fresh Thursday). Follow @tacktackstudio on Instagram or Threads for first looks.
