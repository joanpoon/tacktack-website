Quick facts

Who we are
Tack, tack! is an independent shop in Hong Kong for curated design classics from around the world: furniture, lighting and objects, new and pre-loved.
What's listed now
Pieces from Isamu Noguchi AKARI, DULTON and Heller (Anna Dawson), all brand new. Every price is shown on the site in Hong Kong dollars.
How to buy
Message us on WhatsApp (+852 6507 0791) or email hello@tacktackstudio.com. There's no online checkout: we confirm availability, send extra photos if you like, and share payment details.
Payment
FPS, PayMe, bank transfer or cash, and PayPal for overseas buyers.
Pickup & delivery
Pickup is by appointment (we share the address when you book), or we deliver anywhere in Hong Kong, quoted before you pay.
Holds
Under HK$1,500 we take full payment. From HK$1,500 to HK$9,999, a 30% deposit holds a piece for up to 7 days; from HK$10,000, a 20% deposit (min. HK$3,000) holds it for up to 14 days.
