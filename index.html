How buying works

Three simple steps, all over WhatsApp.

Holds & deposits policy →
  1. 1

    WhatsApp us

    Tap the button on any piece. Your message arrives with the item name, SKU and link, so we can reply quickly with photos, measurements and availability.

  2. 2

    Pay a deposit or in full

    Under HK$1,500 we take full payment; above that, a deposit holds the piece for you. We send payment details on WhatsApp.

    FPSPayMeBank transfer
  3. 3

    Pick up or get it delivered

    Collect by appointment, or we'll quote delivery anywhere in Hong Kong. Large furniture goes by movers, quoted separately.
