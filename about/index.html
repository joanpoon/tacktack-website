About

A small shop for well-made things.

Tack, tack! is an independent Hong Kong shop for curated designer furniture, lighting and objects, new and pre-loved: pieces made to last, that deserve more than one home. We keep an eye out for design classics from around the world, the kind of pieces Vitra, HAY, USM Haller or &Tradition are known for, and for the quietly clever, from Isamu Noguchi's paper AKARI lanterns to Anna Dawson's wave-shaped Swell for Heller. What's in stock changes with every drop.

Everything is listed with honest condition notes and real measurements, and sold person to person over WhatsApp. No cart, no pressure: just ask.

Chosen with care

We pick pieces we'd happily live with ourselves, and tell you the story behind each one.

Honest about condition

A clear five-step grade, notes on every mark, and extra photos whenever you ask.

Simple, fair buying

WhatsApp to enquire, FPS, PayMe, cash or PayPal (for overseas buyers) to pay, first paid first served. No surprises.

Tack, tack! at a glance

Tack, tack! (tacktackstudio on Instagram and Threads) is a small, independent design shop based in Hong Kong. We sell online and over WhatsApp rather than from a shop floor, in English and Traditional Chinese, with new pieces every Thursday.

Quick facts

Who we are
Tack, tack! is an independent shop in Hong Kong for curated design classics from around the world: furniture, lighting and objects, new and pre-loved.
What's listed now
Pieces from Isamu Noguchi AKARI, DULTON and Heller (Anna Dawson), all brand new. Every price is shown on the site in Hong Kong dollars.
How to buy
Message us on WhatsApp (+852 6507 0791) or email hello@tacktackstudio.com. There's no online checkout: we confirm availability, send extra photos if you like, and share payment details.
Payment
FPS, PayMe, bank transfer or cash, and PayPal for overseas buyers.
Pickup & delivery
Pickup is by appointment (we share the address when you book), or we deliver anywhere in Hong Kong, quoted before you pay.
Holds
Under HK$1,500 we take full payment. From HK$1,500 to HK$9,999, a 30% deposit holds a piece for up to 7 days; from HK$10,000, a 20% deposit (min. HK$3,000) holds it for up to 14 days.
New pieces
Every Thursday. Follow @tacktackstudio on Instagram or Threads.

Why "pre-loved"

Good design deserves a second chapter.

The best furniture and lighting outlive trends and flats. Choosing second-hand designer furniture keeps a good piece in use, keeps it out of landfill, and gives someone else the joy of living with it. When it's your turn to move on, tell us: we'd love to hear about it.

Pre-loved and limited design pieces, found with care and passed on in Hong Kong.
